New Paper Published in Fuel on Coal Ash REE Characterization

Our latest paper, "Hyperspectral imaging and analysis of coal ash for rare earth elements," is officially published in Fuel (Elsevier). This study presents a non-destructive hyperspectral workflow (VNIR-SWIR) for identifying rare earth element enrichments in coal ash byproducts[cite: 2, 4].

Field Expedition Karakoram Range, Shigar Valley

Hyperspectral Exploration of LCT Pegmatite Belts

Targeting Lithium-Cesium-Tantalum (LCT) pegmatites in the high-altitude Karakoram Range requires blending remote sensing with rigorous field verification[cite: 2, 4]. By pairing spaceborne PRISMA hyperspectral scenes with ASD FieldSpec Pro contact spectra and pXRF/LIBS analyses, we successfully delineated alteration boundaries in rugged mountainous terrain[cite: 2, 4].

UAV Photogrammetry & Structural Mapping in Ophiolitic Copper Systems

During the copper exploration project with Spatio Engineering, we integrated high-resolution UAV orthomosaics with field discontinuity mapping[cite: 1]. The resulting structural models guided targeting for exploration drilling and ultimately supported client approval for open-pit mining operations[cite: 1].
