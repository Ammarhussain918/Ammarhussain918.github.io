About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the Department of Earth and Atmospheric Sciences at the University of Houston[cite: 1, 2], advised by Prof. Shuhab D. Khan[cite: 2, 4]. My research integrates multi-scale hyperspectral remote sensing (spaceborne, airborne, and laboratory spectra), field geology, geochemistry, and machine learning to improve exploration targeting for critical mineral systems (such as Lithium-bearing pegmatites and Rare Earth Elements)[cite: 2, 4].

Prior to my doctoral studies, I spent over three years in the geotechnical and mineral exploration sector conducting detailed geological and structural mapping, UAV photogrammetry, and slope stability investigations[cite: 1].

Research Focus

Hyperspectral Exploration of LCT Pegmatites
Karakoram Range, Shigar Valley[cite: 4]

Utilizing spaceborne hyperspectral data (PRISMA) integrated with ASD FieldSpec Pro contact spectroscopy, pXRF, LIBS, and XRD to identify spectral alteration halos and target Lithium-Cesium-Tantalum pegmatite mineralization[cite: 2, 4].

Rare Earth Elements (REE) Characterization from Unconventional Sources
Navajo Mine Coal & Fly Ash[cite: 2, 3]

Developing high-resolution VNIR-SWIR hyperspectral imaging frameworks to detect and quantify REE concentrations in coal and combustion byproducts[cite: 2, 4].

Tectonic Geomorphology & Basin Morphometry
Kirthar Fold Belt & Western Himalayan Syntaxis[cite: 2, 3]

Applying DEM-derived hypsometric indices and basin morphometry to assess tectonic deformation, structural control, and hydrogeomorphic hazards[cite: 2, 3].
